- The app is now named Pairdraw everywhere: the editor's window title, menus, and messages in every language, the desktop app, the CLI and its `pairdraw` command, the MCP server, the packages under `@pairdraw/`, the Cloud server's pages and emails, and the documentation, with a new mark, an orange app icon, and new favicons and install icons.
- Pairdraw orange is the default accent color, and the interface follows the system's light or dark appearance by default; an accent or theme you already chose stays as it is. Accent-colored text stays readable on every panel, whatever accent you pick.
- The editor has one top bar with the Pairdraw menu, which holds the app menus in the browser, the document tabs, and the open document's Cloud status, collaborators, zoom, AI chat, preview, Settings, and Share. Double-click the tab in front to rename its document. Rulers are hidden until you turn them on with **View → Rulers** or <kbd>⇧</kbd><kbd>R</kbd>. The sidebar's Toggle UI button is gone; hide and show the panels with **View → Toggle UI** or <kbd>⌘</kbd><kbd>\\</kbd>.
- Colors, corner radii, and shadows are refreshed in both themes: the layers, properties, and chat panels are rounded cards inset from the window's edges on a calmer backdrop, and the floating toolbar is a bordered card. Menus, popovers, and dialogs have rounder corners and softer shadows, the command palette opens as a floating list, and window notices such as missing fonts are inset strips above the top bar.
- Primary buttons, Share, the toolbar's active tool, and toasts are filled in high-contrast ink instead of the accent color, dark in the light theme and light in the dark theme; tooltips use the same ink fill, and toasts appear below the top bar. Toggle buttons that are on show a raised pill instead of an accent outline.
- The side panels have a lighter look: segmented controls switch between Layers and Assets and between Design and Code, with Lint as an icon tab beside them, property fields are taller with rounded corners, and the selected layer and the current page show a soft accent tint. Tabs in dialogs and Settings use the same segmented control and raised pill.
- Home shows its sidebar and documents as cards on a dotted backdrop, with New design as the main action, a larger prompt to start a design, and a preview card for each document. The welcome dialog and Settings use rounded cards, and each of the welcome's two offers has an icon.
- In the AI chat, your messages sit in high-contrast bubbles and replies read as plain text with a copy button under each, the message box is a rounded card with the send button in the accent color, and the agent permission prompt uses the app's standard dialog and buttons.
- AI agents show in violet everywhere, in shared rooms too: their cursors, labels, page markers, and the chat's typing indicator, tool calls, and reasoning.
- On phones, the toolbar, HUD buttons, and bottom sheet have a visible edge in both themes, and the bottom sheet's panel tabs show which panel is open and are announced by name to screen readers. Comment pins have a visible edge in dark mode, and the open thread's details stay readable in the comments list.
- Sharing a document in a live room is no longer part of Pairdraw: the Share popover, joining a room by link, the room's voice call, and the name shown to others are gone. AI agents still show their cursors on the canvas.
- Home no longer offers a new board, and the PowerPoint and draw.io exports are gone from the Export menu and the Slides panel. Boards you already have still open, and PNG, SVG, PDF, HTML, and .fig exports are unchanged.
- Home is one page without a sidebar: choose what to create above the prompt (landing page, mobile app, web app, slides, dashboard, or something else) and the agent gets a guide for that kind; start from a Figma file, a page in the built-in browser, or a screenshot the agent rebuilds; and switch between recent files, drafts, and design systems with tabs.
- The editor opens on the canvas alone, laid out as pen.dev's: the tools stand at the canvas's left with the layers panel's toggle above them, the zoom sits in the bottom right corner, the properties panel comes when a layer is selected and leaves with the selection, and a prompt at the bottom of the canvas sends what you write to the AI chat while the chat is closed. A panel opened from its toggle stays open, and Settings → AI turns the canvas prompt off.
- Pairdraw starts in the dark theme instead of following the system. Settings → General → Theme still offers Light and Auto, and a theme you already chose is kept.
