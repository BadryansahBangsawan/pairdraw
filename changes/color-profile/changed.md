- A Display P3 document draws, blends, and exports in its own color profile: on a P3 display its colors look as designed, PNG exports keep the P3 profile, SVG and PDF exports write its colors as they show, text fills keep their wide gamut, and sRGB documents draw exactly as before.
- A Display P3 document's SVG, CSS, Tailwind, JSX, and token exports keep the colors' wide gamut: SVG writes `color(display-p3 …)`, and the text exports do too instead of flattening to sRGB hex. Its page background, shadows, and noise effects draw in its profile, and the color picker, gradient and mesh editors, and eyedropper loupe preview the color the canvas draws.
- The export panel says when JPEG, WEBP, and PDF export writes a Display P3 document in sRGB, and a `.pen` export says its colors are written as sRGB hex.
