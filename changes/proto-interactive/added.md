- Prototype interactions take Figma's While hovering, While pressing, Mouse enter, and Mouse leave triggers and its Change to and Scroll to actions, frames take Overflow scrolling, and `.fig` files keep all of them through a round trip. A Mouse enter or Mouse leave delay set in Pairdraw is not written to `.fig` yet; one read from a Figma file is kept. Agents set them with `set_reactions` and `update_node`'s `overflow_direction`.
- Play runs Change to on instances of variants, from a click or an After delay set once on the main component's variant, and shows the switched variant without changing the document; an imported Figma interactive component plays the same.
- A click in play counts over a frame's or an instance's whole box, so a button's background runs its interaction as its label does.
