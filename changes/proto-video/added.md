- The CLI (`pairdraw export -f mp4`, `gif`, or `webm`) and the MCP `export_video` tool record a prototype as a video: from a start frame, following its After delay interactions, their Dissolve transitions, and optional scripted clicks on a virtual clock, so the clip is the same on every run. MP4 (H.264) and WebM (VP9) are encoded with WebCodecs in the app; a file exported without the app records a GIF.
