## Layout

- A dark workspace with a narrow sidebar, a top bar for filters and time ranges, and a dense grid of panels on a 4 px rhythm.
- Put the most important numbers in the top row as KPI tiles; charts and tables fill the rest.

## Typography

- A neutral sans at 12–14 px; numbers in tabular figures or the monospace so columns line up.
- Panel titles are small and semibold; values are the largest text on screen.

## Color

- Charcoal background, slightly lighter panels, and light grey text. Blue marks selection, links, and the primary series; amber highlights anomalies and the second series. Use green and red only for up and down.

## Shape and depth

- Small radii and hairline borders; panels separate by surface color, not shadow.

## Imagery and icons

- Charts are the imagery: line, bar, and sparkline with thin strokes and subtle grid lines. Compact 16 px outline icons.

## Components

- KPI tiles with value, delta, and sparkline; tables with sticky headers and monospaced numbers; segmented controls for time ranges; status dots.

## Avoid

- Big hero type, photography, bright full-color backgrounds, and decorative shadows.
