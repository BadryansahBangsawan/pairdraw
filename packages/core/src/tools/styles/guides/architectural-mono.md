## Layout

- Lay out like a drawing set: a strict grid, wide margins, and content anchored to corners with technical labels.
- Mix very large images with very small text; leave big empty areas on purpose.

## Typography

- A neutral grotesque in two weights; a monospace for coordinates, dimensions, dates, and index numbers.
- Labels are small, uppercase, and tracked; headlines are medium size, not shouted.

## Color

- Black on off-white, with a stone tint as the only highlight for selected or hovered states.

## Shape and depth

- Square corners and 1 px hairlines everywhere; crosshairs, tick marks, and frame corners as decoration.

## Imagery and icons

- Architectural or product photography in natural light, often black and white; line drawings, plans, and sections.

## Components

- Project lists as ruled tables (index, name, location, year). Buttons are text with arrows or thin outlined boxes.

## Avoid

- Color accents, rounded shapes, shadows, gradients, and playful illustration.
