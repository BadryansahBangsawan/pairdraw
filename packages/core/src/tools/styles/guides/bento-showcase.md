## Layout

- A grid of tiles in mixed spans (1×1, 2×1, 1×2, 2×2) with equal gaps of 16–24 px; each tile holds one feature, one headline, and one visual.
- Lead with a centred hero, then one or two bento grids; tiles align to a strict 4- or 6-column grid.

## Typography

- A characterful grotesque for headings in semibold; a neutral sans for body. Tile headlines 24–32 px, two lines at most.

## Color

- Light warm grey page with white tiles. A few tiles take the orange or lime as full backgrounds to create rhythm; keep near-black text on them.

## Shape and depth

- Large radii (24–32 px tiles), very soft shadows or none; inner visuals get their own smaller radius.

## Imagery and icons

- Product UI crops, 3D renders, big numbers, and simple charts inside tiles; icons sit in rounded squares.

## Components

- Feature tiles, stat tiles, testimonial tiles, and a CTA tile; buttons are pills.

## Avoid

- Uneven gaps, more than two filled accent tiles next to each other, and long paragraphs inside tiles.
