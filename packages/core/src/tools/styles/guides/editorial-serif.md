## Layout

- Lay pages out like a magazine spread: a wide text column (60–75 characters) with generous outer margins and room for pull quotes and captions.
- Lead with a large headline, a deck (one-sentence standfirst), and a byline; let one full-width image open the story.
- Section changes get space and a thin rule, not boxes.

## Typography

- Display serif for headlines in bold, tight leading (1.1); a text serif for body at 18 px with line height 1.6.
- Small capitals or uppercase sans labels for kickers and categories; italic for captions and emphasis.
- Drop caps or oversized pull quotes are welcome once per page.

## Color

- Warm paper background, near-black ink, and a deep wine accent for links, kickers, and the subscribe action. The peach highlight marks quotes or featured blocks.

## Shape and depth

- Very small radii and flat surfaces. Use rules and whitespace for structure instead of shadows.

## Imagery and icons

- Large documentary or portrait photography with captions; illustrations in a consistent editorial hand. Icons are rare and thin.

## Components

- Article cards: image, kicker, headline, two-line excerpt, byline. Buttons are understated, text-like or outlined.

## Avoid

- Dense UI chrome, bright saturated colors, heavy shadows, and sans-serif body copy.
