## Layout

- Predictable structure: top navigation, left sidebar for apps, content in panels on a light grey canvas.
- Favour density with clear grouping: section headers, two-column forms, and tables that fill the width.

## Typography

- One humanist sans in regular and semibold; body 14–16 px. A matching monospace for IDs, codes, and logs.
- Sentence case everywhere; avoid all-caps except tiny table headers.

## Color

- White surfaces on a cool grey canvas, navy text, and a confident blue for primary actions, links, and selection. Status colors (success, warning, error) stay muted and always come with an icon or label.

## Shape and depth

- Small radii (4–6 px), 1 px borders, and a faint 1–3 px shadow only on menus and dialogs.

## Imagery and icons

- Real people at work, diagrams, and product screenshots; a consistent 16/20 px outline icon set.

## Components

- Data tables with sortable headers, filters, and pagination; tabs; breadcrumbs; form fields with labels above and help text below.

## Avoid

- Playful illustration, saturated gradients, large rounded shapes, and ambiguous icon-only actions.
