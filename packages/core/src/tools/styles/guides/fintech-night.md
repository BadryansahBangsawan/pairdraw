## Layout

- Mobile-first: a balance or key number at the top, quick actions under it, then cards and transaction lists.
- On desktop, a dashboard of cards on a deep navy canvas with clear spacing.

## Typography

- A geometric sans for headings and big numbers in semibold; a neutral sans for body. Figures use tabular numbers and the largest size on screen.

## Color

- Deep navy background and surfaces, near-white text, and acid lime for the single primary action and positive figures. Sky blue is the second series and info color.

## Shape and depth

- Medium radii (10–24 px) and a deep, soft shadow for floating cards; thin borders between list rows.

## Imagery and icons

- Card renders, simple charts, and abstract gradients sparingly; filled or duotone icons in round tiles.

## Components

- Balance cards, transaction rows with merchant icon and amount, segmented controls, and a bottom navigation bar.

## Avoid

- More than one bright color at a time, low-contrast grey text, and decorative clutter around numbers.
