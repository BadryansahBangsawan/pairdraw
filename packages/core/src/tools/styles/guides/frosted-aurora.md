## Layout

- Floating panels over a dark backdrop with large blurred gradient glows (violet, cyan) behind them; plenty of space around each panel.
- A centred hero with a short headline and one call to action; feature panels in a loose grid.

## Typography

- A geometric sans for headings in semibold, slightly tight tracking; a neutral sans for body in near-white and muted lavender-grey.

## Color

- Deep night background, translucent surfaces (surface color at 60–80% opacity), violet for the main action, cyan for highlights and focus. Gradients run violet to cyan.

## Shape and depth

- Medium-large radii (12–28 px), a 1 px light border at low opacity on glass panels, and a background blur of about 24 px on them.

## Imagery and icons

- Abstract 3D shapes, light streaks, and glowing orbs; thin outline icons.

## Components

- Glass cards, gradient-bordered primary buttons, chat-like input bars, and soft glowing focus rings.

## Avoid

- Flat grey panels, too many glows competing, low-contrast text on busy gradients, and heavy black shadows.
