## Layout

- Slow and spacious: one idea per screen, large margins, and centred compositions for key moments.
- Let photography take full-bleed panels; keep text blocks short and narrow.

## Typography

- An elegant display serif for headlines in regular or semibold, generous tracking on small uppercase labels (+12%).
- Body text in a clean geometric sans at 15–16 px in ivory; keep paragraphs to three lines.

## Color

- Near-black backgrounds with ivory text. Gold is precious: use it for thin rules, a monogram, or the single main action, never for large fills.

## Shape and depth

- Square corners, hairline gold or dark-grey borders, no shadows. Depth comes from photography and lighting.

## Imagery and icons

- Moody, low-key product and lifestyle photography with deep shadows. Icons, if any, are hairline.

## Components

- Outlined buttons with uppercase labels; links with a fine underline. Product cards show only image, name, and price.

## Avoid

- Bright colors, busy grids, discount badges, bold sans headlines, and rounded pill buttons.
