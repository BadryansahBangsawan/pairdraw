## Layout

- Blocky and direct: stacked boxes with thick outlines, deliberately offset or rotated elements, and asymmetry.
- Sections can sit on contrasting color bands; it is fine for elements to overlap a little.

## Typography

- A quirky grotesque in bold for headlines at large sizes; the same family for body; a monospace for labels and tags.

## Color

- Cream background, white boxes, black outlines and text. Yellow is the main accent fill; coral is the second. Flat fills only.

## Shape and depth

- 3 px black borders on cards, buttons, and inputs; a hard 4 px offset black shadow with no blur on raised elements. Corners square or slightly rounded.

## Imagery and icons

- Cut-out photos with outlines, stickers, hand-placed stars and arrows; thick-stroke icons.

## Components

- Buttons are outlined boxes with offset shadows that "press" on click; tags in mono caps; cards with a colored header strip.

## Avoid

- Soft blurred shadows, gradients, thin hairlines, and polished corporate symmetry.
