## Layout

- High-energy and dark: big display headlines, angled or skewed bands, and grids of cards like an arcade menu.

## Typography

- A wide display face for headlines in uppercase; a neutral sans for body; a monospace for scores, timers, and stats.

## Color

- Near-black purple background, magenta as the main glow and action, cyan for highlights and secondary actions. Keep text near-white; neon is for edges and accents, not paragraphs.

## Shape and depth

- Small radii (4–12 px), 1 px neon borders, and an outer glow (no offset, 24 px blur) on the focused or primary element.

## Imagery and icons

- Night photography, neon signage, grids, scanlines, and synthwave horizons; sharp outline icons.

## Components

- Glowing primary buttons, stat panels with monospaced numbers, countdowns, and tabbed menus.

## Avoid

- Light backgrounds, pastel palettes, glow on every element, and long body text in neon colors.
