## Layout

- Airy and orderly: wide margins, a calm 2- or 3-column grid, and large photography balanced with short text.
- Group related content on white cards over a pale canvas; leave plenty of room between sections.

## Typography

- A soft geometric sans in regular and semibold; headings modest in size with relaxed line height (1.2); body 16 px.

## Color

- Birch white and pale grey surfaces, charcoal text, and a muted pine green for actions and links. The sage highlight backs tags, selected states, and quiet banners.

## Shape and depth

- Medium radii (6–16 px) and a very diffuse shadow for raised cards; borders in light grey.

## Imagery and icons

- Natural light photography: wood, textiles, plants, landscapes. Simple outline icons with rounded ends.

## Components

- Cards with image, title, and one line of text; pill tags; buttons filled green or outlined; minimal forms with clear labels.

## Avoid

- Saturated colors, harsh black, crowded layouts, and glossy effects.
