## Layout

- Bouncy and friendly: rounded blocks, tilted stickers, and wavy section dividers. Center key messages.

## Typography

- A rounded display face for headings in semibold; a rounded sans for body at 16–17 px. Short, cheerful headlines.

## Color

- Cream background, deep purple text and outlines, hot pink for primary actions, and sunny yellow for highlights and badges. Small mint or sky accents are fine in illustration.

## Shape and depth

- Large radii (12–32 px), pill buttons, 2 px outlines, and a chunky solid shadow (no blur) under raised elements.

## Imagery and icons

- Flat or cartoon illustration, characters, and emoji-like stickers; thick rounded icons.

## Components

- Big pill buttons, cards with colored tops, progress with fun milestones, and badges that look like stickers.

## Avoid

- Sharp corners, grey corporate palettes, thin type, and dense text.
