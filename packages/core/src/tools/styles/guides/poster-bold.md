## Layout

- Treat each section as a poster: one message, set huge, often edge to edge and cropped by the frame.
- Stack text blocks hard against each other; let color fields split the page into bold bands.

## Typography

- A condensed display face in uppercase for headlines at 120–200 px with line height 0.9 and tight tracking; a sturdy sans for everything else.
- Dates, prices, and numbers can be as large as the headline.

## Color

- White and black with a signal red-orange and an electric blue as solid blocks. Put black text on the orange; white text on the blue or black.

## Shape and depth

- No radii and no shadows. Thick 2 px rules and solid blocks carry structure.

## Imagery and icons

- High-contrast photography, often duotone or cut out, overlapping type. Arrows and stars as graphic devices.

## Components

- Buttons are big solid blocks with uppercase labels. Tickers and marquee rows suit dates and line-ups.

## Avoid

- Small, timid headlines, soft pastels, rounded corners, and evenly balanced layouts.
