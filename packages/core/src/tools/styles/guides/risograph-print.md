## Layout

- Zine-like: collage composition, overlapping shapes, and type that runs into images. Slight offsets between colored layers imitate misregistration.

## Typography

- A bold expressive grotesque for headlines, large and tightly set; a plain sans for body in the blue ink.

## Color

- Paper cream background with two "inks": deep blue for text and lines, fluorescent pink for shapes and actions, plus yellow as a third ink. Overlaps can multiply (blend mode Multiply).

## Shape and depth

- Flat; no shadows. Small radii, 2 px lines, halftone or grain textures as image fills.

## Imagery and icons

- Duotone photos (blue and pink), halftone dots, cut paper shapes, and hand-drawn marks.

## Components

- Buttons as pink blocks with blue labels; posters, event cards, and tag stickers.

## Avoid

- Gradients, glossy UI, soft shadows, and more than three ink colors.
