## Layout

- A single reading column of about 680 px with a side rail for the table of contents, notes, or metadata.
- Structure with numbered headings, short sections, and clear hierarchy; keep navigation visible but quiet.

## Typography

- One serif family for headings and body; body 17 px with line height 1.65. Monospace for code, references, and data.
- Footnote markers, citations, and figure captions use the smallest sizes in muted text.

## Color

- White page, near-black text, a scholarly blue for links and the current section. The pale yellow highlight marks notes, definitions, or search hits.

## Shape and depth

- Hairline borders around figures, tables, and callouts; small radii; no shadows.

## Imagery and icons

- Diagrams, charts, and figures with numbered captions take the place of decorative imagery. Simple line icons only where they aid navigation.

## Components

- Callouts for notes and warnings with a left rule; tables with ruled rows and right-aligned numbers; breadcrumbs and previous/next links at the end.

## Avoid

- Hero banners, marketing slogans, stock photography, and anything that competes with reading.
