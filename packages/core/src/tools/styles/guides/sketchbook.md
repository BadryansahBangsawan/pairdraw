## Layout

- Informal and personal: content on paper-like cards, sticky notes slightly rotated, and doodled arrows linking ideas.

## Typography

- A handwritten marker face for headings and annotations; a rounded sans for body so paragraphs stay readable.

## Color

- Warm white paper, pencil-dark text and lines, a pen blue for links and actions, and highlighter yellow for emphasis and notes.

## Shape and depth

- 2 px dark outlines with small irregular radii; a short hard shadow (2–3 px, no blur) gives a cut-paper feel.

## Imagery and icons

- Hand-drawn illustrations, doodles, scribbled underlines, and circled words; sketchy outline icons.

## Components

- Sticky-note cards, checklists with hand-drawn ticks, and buttons that look drawn with a marker.

## Avoid

- Handwriting for long text, perfect geometric polish, gradients, and photographic backgrounds.
