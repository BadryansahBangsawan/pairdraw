## Layout

- A centred container (1200 px) with an obvious hero, then alternating feature rows and a card grid.
- Consistent 8 px spacing; sections separated by space and a light surface band, not lines.

## Typography

- A friendly geometric sans for headings in semibold with slightly tight tracking (−1%); a neutral sans for body at 16 px.
- Keep to three heading sizes per screen; muted text for supporting copy.

## Color

- White and very light grey surfaces, near-black text, and indigo for the primary action, links, and focus. The pale indigo highlight backs badges, selected rows, and icon tiles.

## Shape and depth

- Rounded corners (8 px controls, 12 px cards, 20 px panels) and a soft, low shadow on raised cards and menus only.

## Imagery and icons

- Product screenshots in device or browser frames; 1.5–2 px outline icons in rounded tiles.

## Components

- Primary buttons filled indigo, secondary buttons white with a border; inputs with a 1 px border and an indigo focus ring. Pricing cards highlight one plan with the accent.

## Avoid

- Multiple accent colors, heavy shadows, dense borders on every element, and decorative gradients.
