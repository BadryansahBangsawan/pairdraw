## Layout

- Build on a 12-column grid with fixed gutters; every block starts and ends on a column line.
- Align text flush left, ragged right. Leave whole columns empty instead of centring content.
- Order the page with size and position, not boxes: few containers, no decorative frames.

## Typography

- One grotesque family in two or three weights. Headlines are large, tight (line height 1.0–1.1, letter spacing −2%), and short.
- Body text stays at 16 px on a 24 px baseline; captions and labels use the smallest size in the scale, often in capitals with +4% tracking.
- Use numbers and index labels ("01", "02") as structure.

## Color

- Black, white, and greys do almost all the work. The red marks one thing per view: the main action, an active state, or a key figure.

## Shape and depth

- Square corners, 1 px rules, no shadows. Dividers are full-width hairlines in the border color.

## Imagery and icons

- Black-and-white or strongly cropped photography placed on the grid; simple geometric pictograms with 1.5 px strokes.

## Components

- Buttons are flat rectangles: filled red for the primary action, text with an underline or arrow for the rest.
- Tables and lists rely on rules and alignment, not zebra stripes or cards.

## Avoid

- Rounded cards, gradients, drop shadows, centred paragraphs, and more than one accent color.
