## Layout

- Inviting and tactile: large photography, asymmetric text and image pairs, and sections on cream and warm white.

## Typography

- A soft high-contrast display serif for headlines; a friendly grotesque for body at 16–17 px. Menu-like lists with dotted leaders suit prices.

## Color

- Cream background, warm white surfaces, dark brown text, terracotta for actions and highlights, and olive as a second tone for tags and illustration.

## Shape and depth

- Medium radii (8–24 px), arched image masks, and a warm, soft shadow on cards.

## Imagery and icons

- Food, hands, materials, and natural textures in warm light; hand-drawn botanical illustrations; rounded line icons.

## Components

- Menu cards, booking forms, product cards with origin details, and pill tags.

## Avoid

- Cold blues and greys, neon colors, hard black, and sterile layouts.
