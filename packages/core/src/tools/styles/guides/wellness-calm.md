## Layout

- Slow rhythm: generous spacing, centred intros, and content in soft rounded blocks that breathe.
- Keep pages short and focused on one next step; avoid dense grids.

## Typography

- A soft serif for headings in semibold with relaxed line height; a rounded humanist sans for body at 16–17 px.
- Warm, reassuring tone; sentence case throughout.

## Color

- Sand canvas, warm white surfaces, deep green-grey text, and sage for actions. The sand-rose highlight marks callouts, progress, and illustrations.

## Shape and depth

- Large radii (12–28 px), pill buttons, and organic blob shapes in the background. Flat surfaces with no shadows.

## Imagery and icons

- Calm portraits, nature close-ups, and soft illustrations; rounded line icons.

## Components

- Rounded cards, gentle progress indicators, toggles and chips with large hit areas, and friendly empty states.

## Avoid

- Alarming reds, harsh contrast blocks, sharp corners, clinical blue, and crowded information.
